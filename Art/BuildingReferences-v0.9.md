# Building reference pass — Ferguson, Robinson and Bonta

This pass records the evidence used by the next explorable buildings. A dimension is marked **reference-derived** when it is taken from a published plan or map; it is marked **reconstruction** when a public floor plan was not available and the dimension was proportioned from the exterior reference.

## Ferguson Hall

- The facade uses the Ferguson entrance photograph and the college virtual tour as the visual references.
- Current playable reconstruction: **31.5 m × 12 m × 3 levels**, with a 19 m roof/cupola assembly.
- The exterior proportions and entrance details are reference-based. The floor plan, room contents and exact height remain reconstruction values because no public measured floor plan was found.
- Source: [Albion virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Ferguson%20Student%2C%20Technology%2C%20and%20Administrative%20Services%20Building), [Ferguson entrance photograph](https://albionpleiad.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/IMG_4155.jpg), [renovation photographs](https://albionpleiad.com/2024/09/photos-albion-colleges-summer-additions-renovations-and-refurbishments/).

## Robinson Hall

- Current playable reconstruction: **28.0 m × 13.5 m × 4 levels**, with a central open atrium and balcony rails.
- The four-story atrium is supported by the 1994 architecture report describing Robinson’s complete gut renovation and new four-story atrium. Room positions remain reconstruction values until the college supplies a current floor plan.
- Exterior cues include red brick, white stone trim, front columns, central pediment and dormers. The game labels inferred rooms as reconstructed spaces.
- Sources: [Albion virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Robinson%20Hall), [1994 Robinson renovation report](https://www.usmodernist.org/AIAPGH/AIAPGH-1994-09.pdf), [University of Michigan historic image](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/t/tinder/x-7611/tinder-rppc-015221), [Albion academic catalog](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Albion-College-Academic-Catalog-2023-24.pdf).

## Bonta Admission Center

- Current playable reconstruction: **16.5 m × 11.2 m × 1 level**.
- The low brick building, glazed front lobby, canopy and visitor-facing reception arrangement follow the college tour image and the public description of Bonta as the campus front door. Office and lounge partitions are reconstruction values.
- Sources: [Albion virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Bonta%20Admission%20Center), [Bonta history](https://www.albion.edu/news-article/albion-mourns-the-passing-of-frank-bonta-49-dean-of-admissions-emeritus/), [tour-route report](https://albionpleiad.com/2020/10/tours-change-due-to-covid-19-protocols/).

## Measurement policy

The official campus map establishes placement and orientation, but it is not a surveyed building plan. Each new hall keeps its reference-derived dimensions separate from inferred interior values in code and in this document. Student-facing history panels explain when a room is a reconstruction. Before calling a hall an accurate digital twin, the project still needs permissioned interior photography, floor plans or a measured site survey from the college.

The public research register in [PublicCampusResearch-2026-09](PublicCampusResearch-2026-09.md) records the college’s own floor-plan request process and the newly verified Science Complex facts that will guide the next modeling pass.
