# Public campus research register — 2026-09

This register separates public evidence from inferred game geometry. Public pages can guide the model, but they do not grant permission to copy restricted drawings, private student information or copyrighted media into the game.

## Floor-plan availability

Albion’s Facilities Operations / Design and Construction office says it maintains and updates CAD plans of existing buildings, archives completed project PDFs and accepts floor-plan requests through its Drawing Request Form. The public page lists the office at Ferguson 108 and explains that floor plans can be requested through the campus standards/forms process.

Source: [Design and Construction services](https://www.albion.edu/offices/facilities-operations/design-and-construction/services/).

No public downloadable floor-plan file was located for Ferguson Hall, Robinson Hall or Bonta Admission Center in this research pass. Their current game interiors therefore remain reconstructions until the college supplies permissioned drawings, measurements or interior photography.

## Ferguson Hall

Public sources identify Ferguson as the Student, Technology and Administrative Services Building. The 2025 campus map lists the Business Office, Counseling Services, Human Resources, Office of Belonging, Office of Community Living, Office of the President and Office of Student Development there. A 2024 student newspaper article documents the Counseling Center’s relocation to the first floor and mentions a back-stair approach and frosted glass for privacy.

Sources: [2025 campus map](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ac_campus_map_8-25.pdf), [Counseling Center relocation](https://albionpleiad.com/2024/09/supporting-minds-the-counseling-centers-role-in-student-success/), [official virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Ferguson%20Student%2C%20Technology%2C%20and%20Administrative%20Services%20Building).

Modeling implications:

- Keep a privacy-aware Counseling Center zone on the first floor and label its placement as reference-derived rather than asserting a current office plan.
- Preserve the back-stair interaction as a distinct route in the next interior pass.
- Use the campus map’s service list for history and wayfinding, not as a measured floor plan.

## Robinson Hall

The public 1994 architecture report describes Robinson’s complete gut renovation and a new four-story atrium. The official catalog and tour identify humanities and social-science teaching and office uses. Historic photographs provide facade and portico references, but no current room-by-room plan was found.

Sources: [1994 renovation report](https://www.usmodernist.org/AIAPGH/AIAPGH-1994-09.pdf), [official virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Robinson%20Hall), [2023–24 academic catalog](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Albion-College-Academic-Catalog-2023-24.pdf), [University of Michigan historic image](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/t/tinder/x-7611/tinder-rppc-015221).

Modeling implications:

- The four-level atrium is supported by a public architectural source and remains the strongest verified interior feature.
- Department rooms, elevator position and hidden circulation remain unverified.
- Keep room names generic in gameplay until a current directory or floor plan confirms assignments.

## Bonta Admission Center

The official visit page identifies Bonta as the visitor address at 100 N. Hannah Street. The official catalog places the Admission Office there, and Albion’s history article says the building was named for Dean of Admissions Frank Bonta in 1996. A public campus-design case study describes later Bonta upgrades including signage, storytelling tools and a floor-to-ceiling campus map.

Sources: [official visit page](https://www.albion.edu/visit/), [2023–24 academic catalog](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Albion-College-Academic-Catalog-2023-24.pdf), [Bonta history](https://www.albion.edu/news-article/albion-mourns-the-passing-of-frank-bonta-49-dean-of-admissions-emeritus/), [Bonta visitor-experience case study](https://echodelta.co/case-studies/albion-college/), [official virtual tour](https://albion.college-tour.com/#Bonta%20Admission%20Center).

Modeling implications:

- Add a visitor reception, admissions consultation area, accessible route and a large campus-map information display.
- Treat office partitions and back-of-house areas as reconstruction values.
- Keep the game’s history panel focused on the public visitor role and Frank Bonta’s documented connection.

## Science Complex — next detailed hall group

Albion’s Earth and Environment page says the Science Complex comprises four connected buildings with a four-story atrium and describes extensive mineral, fossil, rock and map collections. The Chemistry page adds that the complex has a 7,000-square-foot atrium, windows into laboratories, research space throughout and a LEED Silver certification. It identifies Kresge Hall as built in 2006 and Putnam Hall as renovated in 2004. The Physics page places Physics, Earth and Environment, and Mathematics and Computer Science in Palenske Hall. Public accessibility and safety pages identify restroom locations, emergency phones and elevator locations that can guide signage and navigation.

Sources: [Earth and Environment facilities](https://www.albion.edu/departments/earth-environment/campus-facilities/), [Chemistry facilities](https://www.albion.edu/departments/chemistry/campus-facilities/), [Physics facilities](https://www.albion.edu/departments/physics/campus-facilities/), [campus resources and restrooms](https://www.albion.edu/offices/belonging/lgbtqia2s-resources/campus-resources/), [emergency telephones](https://www.albion.edu/offices/campus-safety/emergency-information/emergency-telephones/), [classroom venues](https://www.albion.edu/offices/camps-and-conferences/venues/classrooms/).

Modeling implications:

- Build one connected science complex with a shared four-story atrium, then branch to Kresge, Norris, Palenske and Putnam wings.
- Model visible teaching laboratories, hallway windows, the Norris lecture hall and collection displays before inventing private research rooms.
- Include accessible restrooms, elevators, emergency phones and clear room signs in the navigation pass.
- Preserve the public LEED and “Science on Display” story as an educational interaction.

## Public residential plans that can be used later

Albion publicly publishes room and floor-plan information for Wesley Hall, Seaton Hall, Whitehouse Hall, Mitchell Towers, 416 Erie, 507 Erie, Munger Place and the Mae. These are suitable sources for later residential interiors. The Wesley page links a room-dimensions PDF, which is already represented as a separate reference room in the game.
