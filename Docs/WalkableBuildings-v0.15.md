# Walkable campus chapter expansion

The map now has a reusable walkable interior for the main public destinations instead of leaving them as sealed exterior shells. Alongside Ferguson Hall, Robinson Hall, Bonta Admission Center and the Science Complex, the chapter now builds:

- Baldwin Hall
- Goodrich Chapel
- Herrick Theatre
- Kellogg Center / Dickie Hall
- Olin Hall
- Stockwell Memorial Library
- Mudd Learning Center
- Dow Recreation and Wellness Center
- Kresge Gymnasium

Each generated building has a modeled entrance, collision shell, floor plates, room partitions, stairs, lights, furniture and a history body for the H story interaction. The public map destination still provides the exterior arrival point, and the player can walk to the entrance and press **E** to open the door. The same system handles future halls without creating a separate one-off controller.

The interiors are game-scale reconstructions based on the public campus map and tour descriptions. They are playable spaces, not surveyed architectural drawings. The smoke test now requires eleven mapped walkable majors plus the Science Complex and Ferguson interior, and verifies all 61 destination arrivals, parking, campus activities and driving in the same build.
