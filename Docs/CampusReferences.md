# Albion campus references

Reference search: September 6, 2026. These links support research and visual planning. The current environment is an original concept kit, not a measured campus replica.

## Primary references

- [Albion College: Explore Visit](https://www.albion.edu/explore-visit/): college-operated starting point, linking campus visits and describing the interactive map and building imagery.
- [Albion virtual campus experience](https://albion.college-tour.com/): interactive campus tour listing buildings and their photographs. Useful for facade, roofline, and landscape reference. We have not verified complete 360-degree coverage, interiors, or reusable asset licensing.
- [Official campus map, August 2025](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ac_campus_map_8-25.pdf): building names and site layout. Includes Goodrich Chapel, Stockwell Memorial Library, Astronomical Observatory, Bobbitt Visual Arts Center, Baldwin Hall, and the Science Complex/Norris Center.
- [College virtual sneak peek](https://apply.albion.edu/portal/virtual_peek): college-operated remote visit program for campus orientation.

## Visual research leads

- [Michigan Conference campus aerial](https://michiganumc.org/new-president-welcomes-albion-students/): aerial photograph showing campus massing and chapel steeple.
- [Community view of Albion College](https://albionmich.net/albion-college/): photographs useful for seeing chapel scale and landscaping.

The search found images and a campus tour, not an accessible, verified full-campus 360 dataset. No photographs from these sites have been bundled, traced as textures, or claimed as project-owned material. The user's authorization to work on the college project does not identify the ownership or reuse terms of each third-party photograph.

## Reconstruction workflow

1. Establish orientation and relative building placement from the official map. Keep the fictional player-built Legacy Campus separate from the surveyed exploration map.
2. Prioritize a walkable route around the chapel, library, observatory, arts center, and science complex. Confirm current public-access boundaries with the college team.
3. For each building, record a source manifest: facade views, approximate dimensions, date, photographer, permission scope, and missing sides. Build rough massing before detailed facades.
4. Capture all exterior sides, roof references from approved sources, and eye-level panoramas at connecting paths. Include scale references; remove incidental personal information from published assets.
5. Model realistic geometry in Blender, then derive a fantasy variant with compatible footprints and landmark silhouettes. Share collision and navigation structures where practical.
6. Test the campus route in person and from home. No history entry should require an inaccessible or restricted location; provide an equivalent room-play quest.

Student ID is not needed to continue development and should not be committed. Any later permission documentation belongs in an appropriate private project record, not the public game asset folder.

## Historical content standard

The prototype claims only the named buildings/uses supported by the official map. Magical objects, squirrel lore, and memory stories are explicitly fictional. Additional dates, historical people, and events must have direct college/archive citations and editorial review before they become educational game content.

## History cards shipped in Unity 0.4

- [Our History — Albion College](https://www.albion.edu/about/at-a-glance/our-history/): charter in 1835, degree authorization for men and women in 1861, first permanent building cornerstone in 1840 and the Quad.
- [Observatory History — Albion College Department of Physics](https://www.albion.edu/departments/physics/observatory-history/): cornerstone on September 8, 1883; completion in summer 1884; early physics, mathematics and astronomy teaching use.

Reviewed September 7, 2026. Game text paraphrases these sources and links to them inside the history panel. The third card is an original design workshop. The teaching hall and pavilion are original architecture that presents the lessons, not reconstructions of the historic observatory or other named Albion buildings.
