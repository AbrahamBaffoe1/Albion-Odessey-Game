# Next development milestones

The 0.8 update includes a playable three-level Ferguson reconstruction, a rigged student, reusable doors/interior loading, campus textures and trees, and a consolidated interface. This is a prototype milestone, not completion of the full campus.

1. **Ferguson reference and usability pass.** Compare every visible facade and entrance against college references; obtain additional interior photographs or floor plans before describing rooms as exact. Correct scale, signage and room contents. Playtest stairs, doors, history prompts and camera movement on Mac.
2. **Robinson Hall and Bonta Admission Center.** Build distinctive Blender exteriors and connected walkable interiors using the shared door, room and history systems. Mark inferred areas explicitly. Finish and playtest each building before moving to the next hall.
3. **Campus placement and landscape.** Refine building footprints, orientation, roads and pedestrian routes against the published map. Improve trees, planting, benches and lighting while keeping entrances and paths clear. Track which buildings remain approximations.
4. **Student movement and campus life.** Refine clothing fit and animation transitions, connect interaction and jumping animations, and extend the improved character system to campus NPCs. Add believable walking routes and classroom behavior.
5. **Interface and accessibility.** Test readable panels at multiple window sizes, remappable Mac-friendly controls, controller input, captions, volume settings and uncluttered history/media navigation. Preserve the single gameplay HUD.
6. **Building and learning gameplay.** Connect custom buildings, room layouts and course activities more tightly; improve placement validation, saved designs and classroom schedules. Keep local enrollment distinct from official college enrollment.
7. **Online foundation.** Implement accounts, authoritative shared sessions, persistence, permissions and moderation, starting with a small multiplayer campus test. Online play and enrollment are not currently implemented.
8. **VR and release readiness.** Add and test headset navigation, readable world-space panels and media interaction on named supported hardware. Profile performance, complete save/recovery and accessibility checks, and package signed releases where signing credentials are available.

Completion gates for each new hall: an identifiable referenced exterior, a usable entrance, walkable rooms and stairs where applicable, collision and camera checks, history/media interactions, safe return to campus, and measured performance. Full-campus and VR completion must be assessed separately from the count of map pins.
