# Campus activities pass

The chapter now has authored activity spaces that are part of the world geometry, rather than background-only animation:

- **Learn:** Ferguson learning lab desks, Robinson seminar tables and Science Complex lab benches.
- **Teach:** Professor Ayo's instructor station in the Ferguson learning lab.
- **Eat and buy food:** Baldwin Dining Commons has a service counter, checkout, prepared food, dining tables, chairs and plates.
- **Work:** A dining worker and cashier move between the prep and checkout points.
- **Play:** Kellogg Commons has table tennis, a chess table and a lounge bench.
- **Greek-life clubs:** every Greek-life house has an active club floor with a DJ booth, speakers, lighting, lounge seating and dancing students.

Nine named student and staff agents follow short, deterministic routes. They pause at the task point and use the existing rigged student locomotion, including seated poses for study and meals. The spaces are visible and walkable so a player can read the room, approach the station and press **E** or **F** to take part.

Interactions are lightweight and offline-safe: studying, teaching, buying, eating and playing update the in-game notice and increment the activity telemetry used by the release smoke test. Course progress and saves continue to use the existing campus systems.

## Finding them

Use **M** to open the campus map, travel to Baldwin Hall or Kellogg Center, and walk to the signed dining or game lounge annex. Ferguson, Robinson and the Science Complex contain the learning spaces. Aim at a station and press **E** (or **F**) to interact.
