# Albion campus inventory — version 0.6

Reviewed September 7, 2026 against the [official August 2025 map](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ac_campus_map_8-25.pdf). All numbered destinations are represented below, including Bobbitt Annex, four Science Complex wings, and three Davis fields. Map number 56 has two labels for one location; 47 is both Munger Hall and its apartments. The off-map boathouse arrow has no plotted location and is recorded as a research gap rather than invented.

These are exterior game approximations. Relative centers follow the map, while sizes, facades, heights, roads and remote-site distances are approximated. Interior access is not implemented for these new buildings. Legacy Hall remains original fictional architecture.
