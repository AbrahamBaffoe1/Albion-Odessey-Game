# Albion campus expansion — v0.6

This release adds 61 exterior destinations from Albion College's August 2025 campus map, a visible animated Keeper, character creation settings, three drivable open-top cruisers, and seven persistent discoveries.

## Play

The game starts by the Quad. Close the welcome panel to explore.

- **M**: searchable campus directory and clickable map; select a destination and choose **Travel to building**.
- **F3**, or **Escape → Character settings**: choose a preset or create a look with name, five skin tones, five outfit colors, three hairstyles/headwear choices and an optional backpack. A live 3D preview shows the result. Save and play, or close the panel; both save the settings for the active Keeper.
- **V**: switch third-person/first-person. Third-person is the default. The camera moves inward near walls. **WASD/arrows**, mouse, Shift and Space retain walking, looking, running and jumping.
- **E/F** beside a cruiser: enter. **W/S** accelerate/reverse, **A/D** steer, **Space** brake, **E/F** exit once stopped. Exit checks for clear space. Cruisers remain at their last parking spot for the session; they reset to their three spawn points on relaunch.
- **E/F** near a discovery: collect its card. The campus map's **Campus discoveries** button opens the seven-card collection and optional clue travel.
- **Tab**: switch between four local Keeper profiles, each with separate appearances and discoveries.
- **M → Legacy Hall & builder**: reach the original tower, classroom, history pavilion and personal-campus builder. F2 retains building mode.

Character appearance and discovery cards use separate, per-Keeper local preferences; existing version-2 economy/course saves remain compatible. The opt-in automated walkthrough uses a separate preferences namespace and save file.

## Campus scope

See [CampusInventory.md](CampusInventory.md) for the complete roster. All numbered destinations in the map are represented, resolving duplicate labels and splitting the four Science Complex wings and three Davis fields. Buildings have labeled exterior masses, windows and roof forms. The chapel has a bell tower and spire, the observatory a round tower and dome, houses pitched roofs, and athletic fields markings and seating.

The layout, dimensions, facades and remote-site distances are approximate game interpretations of the official map. This is not a surveyed or photogrammetric reconstruction. The new buildings have exterior collisions; their real interiors are not modeled. The original eight-floor Legacy Hall remains walkable. The boathouse is an off-map arrow without a plotted position and remains a research gap. Named subfacilities within buildings (such as Dow's pool/fitness center and Wesley Chapel within Goodrich) belong to their parent exterior, rather than separate invented buildings.

## Discoveries and sources

1. **Brit the Briton**: Albion's mascot, introduced in fall 2011. The game includes an original stylized knight interpretation. [College orientation booklet](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Download-the-booklet-PDF.pdf).
2. **The Rock**: northeast Quad; class of 1899 gift and student painting tradition. [Albion-isms](https://www.albion.edu/about/at-a-glance/albion-isms/).
3. **The observatory telescope**: the historic Clark refractor and sidereal clock. [Observatory history](https://www.albion.edu/departments/physics/observatory-history/).
4. **Rare books**: library holdings include early printed books from the 1400s. [Archives rare books](https://archives.albion.edu/rare-books). [Library locations](https://library.albion.edu/about) places Archives and Special Collections in Mudd.
5. **Whitehouse's living classroom**: visitor center, classroom, wildlife observation and reptile/amphibian exhibits. [Nature Center](https://www.albion.edu/about/our-campus/whitehouse-nature-center/).
6. **The former chapel in Kellogg**: Gerstacker Commons' wooden floor/balcony area was the former chapel. [College orientation booklet](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Download-the-booklet-PDF.pdf).
7. **Pip's golden acorn**: explicitly original game fiction.

Reviewed September 7, 2026. Each factual card links directly to its source. Tokens, discovery positions and artwork are game interpretations, and the player does not take real campus artifacts.
