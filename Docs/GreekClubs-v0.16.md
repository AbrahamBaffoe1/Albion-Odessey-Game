# Greek-life and residential pass

Greek-life houses and the main residential halls are now explorable. The Greek houses share the reusable interior pipeline, then receive a club layer inside the room: a checkerboard dance floor, DJ booth, speakers, lounge seating, colored club lighting and two named dancer agents per house.

The dance agents move between positions, pause on the floor and use a procedural body sway on top of their rigged student locomotion. Every Greek-life house has an **ACTIVE CLUB** sign and an interactable dance floor. Aim at the floor and press **E** or **F** to join the party.

The residential pass covers Briton House, Burns Street Apartments, Dean Hall, Karro Apartments, Mitchell Towers, Munger Hall, Seaton Hall, Wesley Hall and Whitehouse Hall. Each has a modeled entrance, room floors, stairs, common furniture, collision and H history content.

The runtime smoke test now verifies 31 mapped walkable buildings, 11 active clubs and 22 club agents in addition to the existing campus, parking, dining and learning checks.
