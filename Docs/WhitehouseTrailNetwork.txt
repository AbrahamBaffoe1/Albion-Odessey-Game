F7 enters/exits the separate explorable trail world. F6 remains the existing multiplayer runner; its straight race course has not yet been converted to follow this network.

Source: https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/whitehouse-nature-center-trail-map-1.pdf
Map dated 2018-10-02, by Alyssa Olson and Michael McRivette, Albion College Geology Department.

73 vector path pieces, 2187 vertices, six named trail families. Legend strokes excluded. Dashed connectors remain separate pieces, rather than silently inventing missing line segments. Scale inferred from the 0.2-mile bar; visitor origin estimated from the map. Trail width, flat ground and tree placements are provisional. The map is a cartographic source, not a surveyed current terrain model.

Current official Trails page reports McIntyre Marsh closed pending repairs; game signs retain that status. The Rail Trail is a former interurban railway bed, not the operational railroad depicted separately on the map.

Still needed: river/wetland polygons, bridges and boardwalks, elevation data, habitat-specific planting, visitor building and campus connection. Actual race-path following and junction choices are not implemented yet.
