# Campus parking pass

The campus chapter now includes a readable parking layer anchored to Albion's public campus references. The implementation uses three named lots:

- **Bonta visitor parking** beside the admission center
- **Kellogg visitor parking** beside the Kellogg Center
- **Ferguson parking lot** for the academic and event area

Each lot has a paved surface, curbs, marked stalls, an accessible stall, permit signage and parked vehicle props. The vehicles are intentionally unbranded silhouettes covering sedan, crossover, hatchback and pickup shapes so the scene feels lived in without copying a manufacturer's protected design. The three existing drivable campus cars remain available for driving.

The lot names and placement are based on Albion's public visitor-parking guidance and the current public campus map:

- [Maps, directions and parking](https://www.albion.edu/about/plan-your-visit/maps-directions-parking/)
- [Albion College campus map (August 2025)](https://www.albion.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ac_campus_map_8-25.pdf)
- [Theatre productions parking guidance](https://www.albion.edu/departments/theatre/productions/)

This is game-scale placement rather than a survey or construction document. If Albion later supplies a measured site plan, the lot centers, stall spacing and curb footprints can be replaced without changing the reusable parking or vehicle components.

## Verification

The campus smoke test now asserts at least 36 marked stalls and 20 parked vehicles in addition to the existing 61 campus destinations and three drivable cars. Visual smoke captures cover the aerial campus overview and a Ferguson-area driving view so the lots are visible in the shipped scene.
